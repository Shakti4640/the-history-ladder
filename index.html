The History Ladder

Personalities of India & the World — climbing the centuries, fifty years at a time
Each row is a century; the two columns under each region split it into its first half (years 1–50) and later half (years 51–100). A figure sits in the half where most of their life or reign fell — so no one is pushed into the wrong half. Indian rulers are listed first in each cell to trace the unbroken chain of dynasties, with Sufi saints, Governors-General & Viceroys, landmark battles & wars and inventions & discoveries woven into their centuries. Cards sit two-up to pack each rung; hover or tap any card for its story. 647 entries · 298 from India · 349 from the wider world.
